# Prompt guides — questions to ask Claude about your store

Once WooCommerce for Claude is connected, Claude can answer real questions about how your store is doing. The questions below are inspiration — paste any of them into Claude and WooCommerce for Claude will pull the data, do the comparison, and write the answer.

There's no special syntax. Plain English works. The clearer you are about the window ("last 30 days") and what you'd do with the answer ("which one thing should I prioritise this week?"), the sharper the response.

---

## 1. Get the lay of the land

Where your store stands, in plain English.

- *"What does my store sell, who buys, and when do they buy most?"*
- *"Last 30 days vs. the prior 30 — revenue, orders, AOV, and what changed."*
- *"Walk me through my store like I'm a new investor — what's the headline, what's the risk, what's the upside?"*
- *"Show me the day-of-week and hour my customers buy most so I can time emails and ads."*

## 2. Diagnose what changed

Something feels off — find out what.

- *"My last 7 days look soft. What changed? Break it down by product, category, and time of day."*
- *"Which products had the biggest movers up and down this month?"*
- *"Are refunds or failed orders trending up vs. my baseline?"*
- *"Revenue is down month-over-month. Walk me through the most likely causes using my actual data."*

## 3. Find what's actually working

Where to lean in.

- *"Top 10 sellers this month with stock levels and revenue share."*
- *"Which categories are pulling weight and which are dead weight?"*
- *"Did my last promo lift revenue or just shift it from full-price?"*
- *"How much of my revenue is from repeat buyers, and which products bring them back?"*

## 4. Find what needs attention now

What's bleeding today.

- *"What's stuck in fulfillment? Show on-hold orders by age and any failed orders from the last 30 days."*
- *"Which top sellers are running low or out based on sales velocity?"*
- *"Where's the leakage right now — biggest discounts, biggest refunds, and oldest on-hold or failed orders worth chasing?"*

## 5. Tune up the catalog

WooCommerce for Claude's quality layer — worth running once, then quarterly.

- *"Audit my catalog's AI-readiness — where am I losing the most points?"*
- *"Pick my 5 worst-scoring products and draft rewrites for descriptions, titles, and SEO."*
- *"What policy pages, schema, or alt-text gaps are hurting my score?"*

---

## Tips

- **Be specific about the window.** "Last 30 days" or "this quarter" is better than "recently" — Claude will pick a reasonable default if you don't, but exact windows give you cleaner comparisons.
- **Ask follow-ups.** A good WooCommerce for Claude answer surfaces 1–2 follow-up questions. Click those.
- **Say what you'd do with the answer.** "What changed last week, and which one thing should I do about it?" steers Claude toward an action, not a report.
- **Date-range gate.** Queries spanning more than 365 days require an explicit confirm step — that's deliberate, to keep your store fast. Claude will surface a cost estimate; reply yes/no.
